# How to Configure macOS for Privacy & Security

* Minimize personal identifiable and private data being sent to Apple and other third-parties.
* Prevent unauthorized access to your devices and data.
* Minimize tracking across the internet and physical locations.

## Don't sign into your Apple or iCloud accounts

Consider using your Mac without being signed into your Apple and iCloud accounts.

## Turn on firewall protection

1. Open System Settings, click Network  in the sidebar, then click Firewall.
2. Turn on Firewall.
3. Click Options.
4. Toggle Block all incoming connections on.
5. Click OK.

## Change the name of your Mac

1. Open System Settings, click General in the sidebar, then click About.
2. Edit the Name of your Mac.
3. Press Enter.

By default, your Mac is identified by your first name on the network. Choose something less identifiable.

## Disable recent documents, applications & servers in Meny Bar

1. Open System Settings, click Menu Bar in the sidebar.
2. Set Recent documents, applications, and servers to None.

## Spotlight

1. Open System Settings, click Spotlight in the sidebar.
2. Disable Help Apple Improve Search.
3. Scroll down to the bottom, click Search Privacy...
4. Add any locations on your Mac that you don't want to be indexed by Spotlight, using the + sign.
5. Click Done.

## Lock Screen

1. Open System Settings, click Lock Screen in the sidebar.
2. Set Require password after screen saver begins or display is turned off to Immediately.

## Disable Location Services

1. Open System Settings, click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, click Location Services.
2. Disable Location Services.

## Turn off Applie Intelligence Report

1. Open System Settings, click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, click Apple Intelligence Report.
2. Set Report Duration to Off.

## Enable FileVault

1. Open System Settings, click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, click FileVault.
2. Enable FileVault.
3. Only allow resetting the password with a local Recovery Key.

## Set a strong password for your Mac

1. Open System Settings, click Touch ID & Password in the sidebar.
2. Under Password, click Change.
3. Type in your Old Password.
4. Type in a strong New Password and Verify it.
5. Leave Password Hint blank.
6. Click Change Password.

Consider using Diceware to generate a strong password that is easy to remember.

## Disable AirDrop & Handoff

1. Open System Settings, click General in the sidebar, then click AirDrop & Handoff.
2. Disable Handoff between this Mac and your iCloud devices.

If you are not using AirDrop frequently, then disable it by setting AirDrop to No One.

## Require an administrator password to access system-wide settings

1. Open System Settings, click Privacy & Security in the sidebar, scroll down, then click Advanced.
2. Require an administrator password to access system-wide settings

Consider setting Log out automatically after inactivity.
